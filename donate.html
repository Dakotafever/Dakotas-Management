A few things to know

Frequently asked questions

Where does my contribution go?

Contributions help fund project-related costs such as hosting, servers, domains, development equipment, storage, and content creation. They may be used across different projects depending on current needs.

Do I have to donate?

Not at all. Contributions are completely optional. Following, sharing, testing, and giving feedback on projects are also valuable ways to help.

Is there a minimum amount?

The current checkout starts at $1 USD. You can enter a custom amount or choose one of the suggested amounts.

Who processes the payment?

PayPal handles the payment. This page does not collect or store your payment details.

Is this a purchase or a subscription?

No. This page is for optional one-time contributions. It does not provide a product, guarantee a reward, or start a recurring subscription.
